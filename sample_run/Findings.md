**What is happening:** Freelancers are facing financial strain due to high commission fees on major platforms like Fiverr and Upwork. Additionally, those performing international work are struggling with cumulative transaction and currency conversion fees charged by mainstream payment processors like PayPal.

**Why now:** As the gig economy expands and cross-border collaboration becomes more common, the cumulative impact of these fees is increasingly visible as a direct deduction from freelancers' net income.

**Business angle:** Develop a specialized payment processing tool or an invoicing platform that offers lower transaction rates for independent contractors and features integrated currency conversion at transparent, near-market rates.
